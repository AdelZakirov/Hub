# Personal Budget Agent Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 September 2026_

## Scope

Personal Budget Agent is a private, non-commercial application used solely by its owner to access and analyse the owner's own bank account information for personal budgeting.

It is not offered to other users and does not provide financial services to third parties.

## Data processed

The application may process account information made available through the owner's bank and Enable Banking, including:

- account identifiers and account metadata;
- balances;
- transaction dates, amounts, descriptions and counterparties.

## Purpose

Banking data is processed only to:

- import and review the owner's transactions;
- categorise personal spending;
- monitor personal budgets and cash flow;
- produce personal financial summaries.

## Storage and sharing

Banking data is intended to be stored only in systems controlled by the owner for personal use.

The application does not sell banking data, use it for advertising, or intentionally disclose it to third parties. Data necessarily passes through the bank and Enable Banking when using their account-information services, subject to their own privacy terms.

## Retention

The owner controls retention and deletion of locally stored data and may delete it at any time.

## Contact

Questions about this application can be directed to the owner through the GitHub profile associated with this repository.
