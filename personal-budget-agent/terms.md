# Personal Budget Agent Terms of Use

_Last updated: 18 September 2026_

## Purpose

Personal Budget Agent is a private, non-commercial personal finance tool used solely by its owner.

Its purpose is to access and analyse the owner's own bank account information for personal budgeting, transaction categorisation and financial summaries.

## No public service

The application is not offered to third parties, does not accept external users and does not provide commercial banking, payment, investment, accounting or financial-advice services.

## Account access

The application may connect to the owner's own bank accounts through Enable Banking or another authorised account-information provider.

Any bank authentication and consent remain subject to the terms and security requirements of the relevant bank and account-information provider.

## Responsibility

The owner is responsible for keeping application credentials and private keys secure and for verifying any financial information or analysis produced by the application.

The application is intended only as a personal organisational tool and should not be treated as professional financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

## Changes

These terms may be updated by the owner as the application changes.
